2. Branch Creation & Custom Pricing Approval Workflow

To preserve marketplace quality and prevent predatory pricing or bogus branch listings, changes adhere to a strict audit loop:

  1. Owner creates a new physical branch or enables Doorstep delivery in a new district.
  2. Owner maps services from Master Catalog and sets custom prices:
    Validation: Custom Price ≥ Regional Minimum Floor Price
  3. Branch status remains PENDING_INSPECTION and does not appear on customer search.
  4. Zonal Manager inspects the location via digital map verification or field audit.
  5. Upon approval, the branch and pricing matrix immediately synchronize across search caches.

4. Doorstep Logistics & Commute Buffer Algorithm

For home salon and bridal bookings (e.g., in coastal Puduvalasai or Panaikulam):

  • Coverage Radius Check: Customer delivery GPS coordinates must be ≤ Stylist's doorstep_radius_km from the branch origin.
  • Mandatory Commute Buffer: The system automatically blocks a 30-minute travel buffer before and after the service duration to prevent stylist arrival delays.
  • Live ETA Tracking: Stylist triggers "On the Way" in mobile app → Customer receives real-time tracking link with live map and estimated arrival time.

6. Rescheduling & Tiered Cancellation Workflow

Customers can self-service manage bookings directly from the app:

  • Rescheduling: Permitted up to 2 hours prior to slot start time (max 2 reschedules within 14 calendar days). The engine automatically releases the old slot and re-locks the new slot.
  • Cancellation & Refund Rules:
    • > 12 Hours: 100% full instant refund to payment method / wallet.
    • 4–12 Hours: 80% refund (20% fee; 15% credited to Salon Owner to compensate reserved slot).
    • 1–4 Hours: 50% refund (50% fee; 40% credited to Salon Owner).
    • < 1 Hour / No-Show: 0% refund (85% paid to Salon to protect barber wages, 15% platform commission).
