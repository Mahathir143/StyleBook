1. Executive Summary & Flow Verification

Your platform requirements for StyleBook establish a complete marketplace ecosystem connecting Management, Salon Owners (Sellers), and Customers (Buyers). The platform is not sold as standalone individual salon software; it functions as a multi-tenant marketplace with membership subscriptions and transaction commissions.

Flow Verification Result: The foundational user hierarchy is conceptually sound, but requires three major operational adjustments to be production-ready:

  1. Role Segregation within Management: Instead of one person holding all management permissions, responsibilities are cleanly bifurcated into Operational Admins (geography, branch approvals, notices) and Finance Officers (commissions, wallet settlements, tax, payouts).
  2. Strict Approval State Machines: When an owner modifies services, pricing, or creates a new branch, it cannot go live immediately. It enters a PENDING_REVIEW state until approved by the territorial Zonal Manager.
  3. Double-Entry Accounting & Escrow Protection: To support both Online payments and Pay-at-Salon cash appointments, the platform must maintain an escrow engine and a pre-funded owner wallet mechanism to guarantee commission collection on cash appointments.

5. Full Double-Entry Accounting & Wallet System

The Financial Workflow: Online vs. Pay-at-Salon Cash

A major challenge in salon marketplaces is securing platform commissions when customers pay cash directly at the counter. StyleBook solves this through a dual-mode ledger:

Case A: Customer Pays Online

  1. Customer pays ₹1,000 via UPI/Card.
  2. Funds held in StyleBook Platform Escrow.
  3. Booking completed via 6-digit OTP.
  4. Platform auto-splits:
    • ₹882 credited to Owner Virtual Ledger.
    • ₹100 Platform Commission (10%).
    • ₹18 GST on Commission (18%).
  5. Owner receives scheduled bank payout (T+3).

Case B: Customer Pays Cash at Salon

  1. Customer hands ₹1,000 cash to the barber.
  2. Barber verifies OTP to complete booking.
  3. Platform deducts commission from Owner Prepaid Wallet:
    • Wallet debited -₹118 (₹100 Commission + ₹18 GST).
  4. Negative Balance Safety Gate: If owner wallet falls below -₹2,000, new online bookings are paused until wallet is topped up.
Financial Split Breakdown (Example: ₹1,000 Booking with 10% Commission):
Gross Booking Total: ₹1,000.00
Platform Commission (10%): ₹100.00
GST on Commission (18% of ₹100): ₹18.00
Net Owner Payable: ₹882.00

7. Real-Time Scenarios & Edge Case Solutions

Operational Resilience Matrix

  • Stylist Sudden Sickness / Emergency Leave:
    System activates the Emergency Reassignment Pipeline. It queries all certified available stylists at the branch and reassigns with 1-click customer confirmation, or issues an instant full refund + apology credit if no staff member is free.
  • Doorstep Commute Buffer & Live Tracking:
    Doorstep bookings automatically inject a mandatory 30-minute travel buffer between appointments. If stylist's GPS indicates travel delay, an automated ETA slip notification is dispatched to the customer.
  • Simultaneous Slot Booking Race Conditions:
    When a customer reaches checkout, the chosen slot is placed under an 8-minute atomic cart lock in Redis/PostgreSQL. If payment is not completed within 8 minutes, the slot is immediately released to the public pool.
  • Service Chaining for Multi-Service Appointments:
    When customer selects Haircut (30m) + Beard Trim (15m) + Facial (45m), the engine requires a continuous 90-minute block for a single stylist, rather than splitting across disjointed times.
