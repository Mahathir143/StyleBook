StyleBook - Software Requirements Specification (SRS) v2.0

Project: StyleBook Enterprise Salon & On-Demand Grooming Platform • Scope: Multi-Tenant Marketplace with SaaS Membership & Booking Commission Model

1. Executive Product Vision & Core Actors

StyleBook is not sold as standalone individual salon software. It operates as an on-demand, regional, multi-tenant grooming marketplace connecting three core user groups:

  • 1. Management (Platform Administrators & Operators): Manages platform compliance, regional onboarding approvals, marketing announcements, territorial pricing boundaries, membership plans, commission structures, and financial payout reconciliations.
  • 2. Salon Owners (Sellers / Service Providers): Can be multi-branch salon enterprises, standalone boutiques, or independent solo/mobile stylists. They configure branches, styler rosters, service catalogs, custom pricing, and shift timings. All branches and services must be approved by Management before going live.
  • 3. Customers (Buyers / Consumers): Self-service appointment discovery (by locality, brand, stylist, or service), real-time booking with 8-minute cart locks, rescheduling, cancellation with refund preview, 6-digit OTP verification at check-in, and dual review submission.

2. Functional Role Separation & Scoped Governance

Instead of a single administrator having omnipotent control, StyleBook separates administrative tasks functionally and territorially:

Role Administrative Scope Functional Responsibilities Permissions
Super Admin Global Platform Master countries, global platform policies, baseline price ceilings, master service categories. Full Read/Write across all tenants and ledgers.
Zonal / Regional Manager Assigned State / Zone / District Onboarding inspection, salon verification, branch approvals, staff sanitation audits, compliance notices. Approve/Reject Salons & Branches in Zone; Toggle Active/DeActive; Issue 48hr compliance notices.
Finance & Accounts Manager Regional / Platform Financials Membership plan setup, commission rate overrides, escrow balance audits, payout batch execution, GST tax invoices. Manage Wallets, Approve Bank Payouts, Audit Disputes, Withhold TDS under Section 194-O.
Brand Manager Assigned Enterprise Brand (e.g. Royal Classic) Multi-branch oversight, staff transfers between branches, enterprise promotional campaign creation. Cross-branch reporting and aggregate staff roster management.

3. Targeted Announcement & Notice System

Management can broadcast announcements and notices filtered by granular target criteria:

  • Target Audiences: MANAGEMENT, SALON_OWNER, BRANCH_MANAGER, STYLIST, CUSTOMER.
  • Targeting Scope: Global • Country • State • Zone • District • Locality • Specific Brand • Single Branch.
  • Notice Types:
    • Informational: Platform updates, new features, seasonal announcements.
    • Regulatory / Compliance: Sanitation audit directive, pricing policy warning, unverified staff flag (carries 48-hour grace period with mandatory owner acknowledgment).

4. Salon Owner Lifecycle & Strict Approval Workflow

4.1 Business Models Supported

  1. Multi-Branch Salon Chains: Central owner account with distributed branch physical stores and centralized billing.
  2. Independent Standalone Salons: Single storefront, local staff, custom operating hours.
  3. Independent Freelance / Doorstep Stylists: Mobile beauticians with no storefront, operating exclusively within a defined kilometer radius (e.g., 10–15 km around Ramanathapuram, Puduvalasai, Panaikulam).

4.2 Approval Gate State Machine

Owners can draft their branches, configure service pricing, and map stylists at any time. However, the system enforces:

Entity Status: Draft → Pending_Approval → Zonal Inspection → Approved → Live on Marketplace

If an owner changes a branch address, toggles doorstep eligibility, or onboard a new stylist, the modification enters an approval queue in the Zonal Manager's dashboard before reflecting on the public customer app.

5. Customer Appointment Engine Requirements

5.1 Discovery & Search Relational Matrix

  • Branch-First Flow: Customer picks Branch → Only services offered at that branch display → Only stylists stationed at that branch certified for the service display (plus "Any Available Stylist" option).
  • Stylist-First Flow: Customer picks Stylist → Only services that stylist is certified to perform display → Only branches where that stylist works display (plus Doorstep option if enabled).
  • Doorstep Validation: Both the service must be marked is_doorstep_eligible = true and the stylist/branch must have is_doorstep_enabled = true within the customer's delivery locality.

5.2 Slot Reservation & Concurrency

  • Atomic Cart Hold: Upon slot selection, the engine locks the time slot for **8 minutes**. If checkout is not completed within 8 minutes, the lock expires and slot returns to public availability.
  • Sequential Duration Chaining: Multiple services booked in one cart (e.g., Haircut 30m + Beard Trim 15m) are dynamically chained into a continuous 45m block.
  • Doorstep Commute Buffer: Doorstep bookings automatically add a mandatory **30-minute travel buffer** before and after the appointment slot.

5.3 Reschedule & Cancellation Policy Engine

Action Time Window Customer Policy Salon Compensation
Reschedule ≥ 2 Hours prior Allowed up to 2 times within 14 calendar days. Price difference adjusted. Slot re-opened immediately on salon calendar.
Cancellation > 12 Hours prior 100% Full Refund. No fee; slot available for new bookings.
Cancellation 4 to 12 Hours prior 80% Refund (20% cancellation fee). 15% paid to Salon to cover reserved slot loss.
Cancellation 1 to 4 Hours prior 50% Refund (50% cancellation fee). 40% paid to Salon to cover idle staff time.
Cancellation < 1 Hour / No Show 0% Refund (100% forfeiture). 85% paid to Salon to safeguard barber wages.

6. Commercial Monetization & Double-Entry Accounting Requirements

6.1 Dual Revenue Engine

  1. Owner SaaS Membership Plans:
    • Starter Solo: ₹799/month (1 branch, 2 stylists, 12% booking commission)
    • Pro Growth: ₹2,499/month (up to 3 branches, 10 stylists, 8% booking commission)
    • Enterprise Chain: ₹5,999/month (unlimited branches & stylists, 5% booking commission)
  2. Platform Booking Commission: Tiered take-rate deducted automatically on completed bookings.

6.2 Cash-At-Salon Protection via Pre-Funded Security Wallets

When customers choose Pay-at-Salon (Cash), the salon receives 100% of the funds in hand. The platform automatically debits the commission + 18% GST from the Owner's Prepaid Security Wallet. If an owner's balance drops below negative_credit_limit (-₹2,000), booking acceptance is paused until topped up.

6.3 Double-Entry Ledger & Payouts

Online payments are held in Platform Escrow. Upon 6-digit OTP verification at check-in, funds are released to the Owner Virtual Ledger. Bi-weekly automated payouts transfer settled funds directly to the owner's bank account via IMPS/NEFT with 1% TDS withheld under Section 194-O.

7. Dual Rating & Feedback Integrity

  • Allowed only after appointment status reaches Completed.
  • Requires two independent 1–5 star ratings:
    1. Salon Branch Rating: Ambience, cleanliness, equipment hygiene, waiting time.
    2. Stylist Rating: Cutting skill, friendliness, attentiveness, styling finish.
  • Database enforces UNIQUE(booking_id) to eliminate fake or duplicate reviews.
  • Owners can post official verified responses to customer reviews.
